**Never played before? You're exactly who we're looking for.** Most DUF players picked up
a disc for the first time at Durham.

- **Everyone welcome** — mixed-gender and mixed-ability, whatever your experience
- **Training** — several sessions a week, indoors and outdoors, with split-gender and mixed teams
- **Beginners Tournaments** — run in Michaelmas term to give new players a first taste of competition
- **Sign up** — create a [UK Ultimate JustGo account](https://ukultimate.justgo.com/Workbench/i/r/public/EventsAndBookingsPublic) to register for events

For session times, follow [@durhamultimatefrisbee on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/durhamultimatefrisbee/).
