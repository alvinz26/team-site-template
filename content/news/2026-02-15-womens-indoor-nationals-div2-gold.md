---
title: Women's team crowned Division 2 indoor national champions
date: 2026-02-15
summary: Durham 1 won gold at the 2026 University Women's Indoor Nationals, Division 2, in Coventry.
---

Durham's women's team are **national champions**. At the BUCS / UK Ultimate University Women's
Indoor Championships, Division 2, held on 14–15 February 2026, Durham 1 took **gold**, ahead of
Surrey 1 (silver) and Stirling 1 (bronze).

Congratulations to everyone involved!
