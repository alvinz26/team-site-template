date: 2026-10-04

Our squad trials are **open to every ability**, and we especially encourage you to come along
if you've never played before.

What we look for:

- **Commitment**
- **Coachability**
- **Athleticism**

Trial sessions are not invite-only, and there are plenty of open-to-all sessions throughout
the year as well. See the training times on the home page.
