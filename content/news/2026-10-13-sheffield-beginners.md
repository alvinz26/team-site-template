---
title: A weekend at Sheffield Beginners
date: 2026-10-13
summary: DUF sent a squad to the experienced division of Sheffield's beginners tournament and finished 16th.
---

DUF travelled to **Sheffield Beginners** for a weekend of indoor frisbee, entering a squad in
the experienced division and finishing **16th**.

It was a great tournament with brilliant vibes and some spectacular plays. Thanks to everyone
who played — including those who pushed through feeling unwell — to the players from other
teams who helped us out, and to the University of Sheffield club for hosting.
